# What's new in FinatriX

25 Nov 2026. Written for users. Where a feature needs a particular phone, it says so. Anything that has not been tried on real hardware is marked **not yet tried on a phone**, so nobody is promised more than exists. The checks the owner still has to run are in [DEVICE_CHECKLIST.md](DEVICE_CHECKLIST.md).

## Travel, money and sharing

- **Travel mode.** Start a trip, spend in another currency and see it in rupees. Rates are live, show their date, and fall back to the last rate when you are offline. Set a trip budget and log bookings made before you leave.
- **Groups.** Budget together and split costs with family and friends. Join by code, see changes live, settle up, and have your share land in your own tracker. Balances always add up to zero. Parents can add children as names, groups can have savings goals, and a trip group uses your trip's currency. A "new" badge shows what changed since you last looked. Push notifications for group activity are **not built yet**.
- **Splits with people who are not on FinatriX.** Track money lent and borrowed with anyone. It never counts as spending.
- **Hide amounts.** One switch hides every amount on screen.

## Know what is coming

- **Subscription radar and bill reminders.** Spots repeating charges and nudges you before bills are due.
- **Monthly money story.** A short, plain summary of your month.
- **Money calendar.** Bills, paydays and renewals on one calendar.
- **Warranty and bill locker.** Keep bills and warranty dates in one place. Photos stay on your phone and are not uploaded.

## Getting things in

- **Share to FinatriX.** Share a statement screenshot or a bill photo from another app straight into FinatriX. Receipts fill the add form for you. The photo is read on your phone. Android is built; iPhone needs one more store setup step before it is in the next build.
- **Home-screen widget.** Safe-to-spend on your home screen, with a button to add an expense. The lock-screen amount stays hidden until you turn it on. **Not yet tried on a phone.**

## Voice and AI

- **Voice assistant** in 11 languages: talk to FinatriX AI, hear answers, and say things like "add 200 for coffee". Every change shows a confirm card first. It listens only while the app is open and you are in a voice conversation. On iPhone, Apple's recogniser covers English and Hindi; the other languages use the keyboard's dictation. **Not yet tried on a phone.**
- **Start it by asking Siri or Google.** "Hey Siri, talk to FinatriX." On Android, the launcher shortcuts work now; the Google Assistant version works for the Play Store build only. **Not yet tried on a phone.**
- **Private on-device AI.** On iPhones with Apple Intelligence and on phones with Gemini Nano, short questions are answered on the phone and nothing is sent. Long chats still use the cloud. FinatriX AI explains and ranks; the numbers always come from your own data and the app's calculators. **Not yet tried on a phone.**

## Your phone

- **App lock.** Lock FinatriX with Face ID, a fingerprint or your screen lock. The app switcher hides your numbers.
- **Apple Watch.** A complication and a small app with safe-to-spend. **Not yet tried on a watch.** Wear OS is not built.

## Not in this release

- Push notifications for groups (needs store keys).
- Wear OS app.
- Indian-language app (dropped).
